Emailing Your Case Load

8 min read · Sending one email to everyone you personally work with

Your case load already lives in Virtuous as an Organizational Group. This walks through turning that group into an email that goes out from you, personally, to the partners on it, and that respects the preferences they have set.

→ What a case load is in Virtuous

The Idea Behind It

Most email tools have one concept: a list. You put people on it, you email the list. Virtuous splits that into two separate things, and almost every mistake people make with Virtuous email comes from treating them as one.

Required on every email
Email List

"What kind of mail is this?"

A subscription channel. It is the thing a partner unsubscribes from. If someone opts out of your updates, they are opting out of this list, not out of everything TMC sends.

The name and description are visible to donors on the subscription preferences page.

How you pick recipients
Contact Query

"Who gets it this time?"

The audience for this one send. It is a live filter: it asks Virtuous, at send time, who is currently on your case load and eligible to be emailed.

Internal only. Donors never see query names.

The list is the channel. The query is the audience. You need both, and you build the query first.

What you're actually building
Build these before you open the wizard
Your case load → Contact Query
Email List
Then work through the wizard
Setup → Design → Target → Specify → Send

The Email List is attached at Setup. The Contact Query is chosen at Target. Both need to exist before you start.

Before you start

Create the Email List at least 15 minutes before you send. Virtuous needs time to generate it, and a list created moments before a send can fail. If you are building the list and the email in one sitting, do the list first and the wizard second. The writing will eat the 15 minutes on its own.

Step 1: Build the Query

This is the step that decides who receives the email, so it is worth doing carefully. Open Query Tool from the left sidebar and start a new Contact Query.

Skip this line if the email is purely informational: a ministry update, a thank-you, a prayer request. Include it the moment there is an ask of any kind.

All conditions must be true at once: on my case load, and not tagged No email, and not tagged No appeals. "Or" here would email your entire database.

Does the count roughly match the size of your case load? If it is dramatically larger, a condition is wrong. Stop and check before going further. This is your last cheap chance to catch a mistake.

Something like Case Load – [Your Name] – Emailable. Reuse this same query for every future send. Partners added to your case load next month will already be in it, and anyone who asks to stop receiving email drops out automatically, without you having to remember them.

Step 2: Create the Email List

Go to Marketing → Email, open the Actions menu in the top right, and choose Manage Lists, then Create a List.

List: the channel your partners subscribe to
1
Name it for the donor, not for the database
The name and description both appear on the subscription preferences page your partners see. Write them the way you would write a magazine subscription line, not an internal label.
2
Write a description that says what they will get
One sentence. Frequency and content. This is what a partner reads when deciding whether to stay subscribed.
3
Leave "Default" off unless it belongs to everyone
Default lists show on the preferences page for every recipient. A personal case-load list is not that.
4
Save, then wait 15 minutes before sending anything

These are internal names. Partners would see them and wonder what a case load is, or why they are on a list called v2.

Make one list, not one per send

It is tempting to create a fresh list for each email. Don't. TMC is capped at 200 email lists total, a one-off list clutters the preferences page your partners see, and unsubscribes recorded against a throwaway list teach us nothing.

Create one list per case-load worker and reuse it for every send. Over time it becomes a real record of who hears from you.

In the Wizard

From Marketing → Email, choose Actions → Create an Email, then pick the visual builder. The wizard runs across five steps shown along the bottom of the screen, and the rest of this guide follows them in order.

Setup

Name Internal only, never seen by partners. Date plus purpose works well: 2026-08 Case Load Update – [Your Name]

Segment Optional. Links this email to a campaign so giving it inspires gets tracked. Use it if this send belongs to a campaign; leave blank for a personal check-in. → Segment codes

Set From Name and From Email to you, every time. Your partners know you, and they have no relationship with a generic ministry address. A name they recognize, with a reply address that reaches that same person, is the whole point of having case loads in the first place.

Design

The builder has three tabs on the right: Content, Rows, and Settings. Work right to left. Settings first for fonts and background, then Rows for the layout, then Content to fill it in. Setting up the design after you have written everything means redoing work.

Write it

Drag a Paragraph block in and type. Click inside it and the toolbar appears, including two things worth knowing:

  • Merge tags personalize the text, most commonly with the recipient's first name.
  • Special Links insert unsubscribe options. If you add none, Virtuous puts a global unsubscribe link in the footer for you.
A note on format

A case-load email is a personal note, not a newsletter. Heavy design works against you here. A plain message with a real greeting and a real signature reads like it came from a person, because it did. Save the multi-column layouts and hero images for organizational sends.

Preview and test

Use Preview under the Actions menu to see desktop and mobile, and send yourself a test email to confirm it renders properly.

Expected in test emails

Merge tags and your custom From Name will not populate in a test send. A greeting that reads as a blank or a placeholder, and a test that arrives from the default TMC sender, are both normal. Check the layout and the wording in a test; trust the merge tags to fill in on the real send.

Publish before you send

Publishing is a separate action from sending, and skipping it is the most common way a case-load email goes out wrong.

Think of publishing as sending a document to the printer. You can keep editing the file afterward, and your screen will show those edits, but the page in your hand is the version you printed. Virtuous always sends the most recently published version. Edits made after publishing stay in the editor and never reach anyone.

The rule

Fixed a typo? Publish again. Every time you change anything after publishing, publish again before you send. When in doubt, publish. There is no penalty for publishing twice.

Target

Click Next to reach Target. Virtuous offers three ways to choose recipients.

OptionWhat it does
Send to Email List Emails everyone currently subscribed to the list. "Show Advanced Options" lets you narrow it with a query.
Use Contact Query Use this Emails the people your query returns, and lets you choose which individuals within each household receive it.
Use Individual Query Emails specific individuals rather than contacts. Not what you want for a household-based case load.

Choose Use Contact Query and select the query you saved in Step 1. Virtuous then asks which individuals within each contact should receive it.

Primary, Primary and Secondary, or All

A Contact in Virtuous is usually a household, and a household can contain more than one person. This setting decides who inside it gets the email. In every case Virtuous uses each person's primary email address. Other addresses on their record are never used for marketing email.

SettingWho receives itWhen to use it
Primary Only the primary individual on the contact record. One message per household, to the person listed first. Misses a spouse who also knows you.
Primary and Secondary Usual pick The primary individual, plus the secondary individual where one has an email address on file. Reaches both spouses in a household. The right default for case-load work, where the relationship is usually with the couple.
All Every individual listed under the contact record. Can pull in adult children or other members attached to the record. Broader than most personal outreach calls for.
Worth knowing

Sending by Contact Query automatically adds everyone who receives the email to the Email List you chose at Setup. That is a feature, not a side effect: it is how your list builds itself over time, and how unsubscribes get recorded against the right channel.

Specify

The Specify step asks when it goes out. Send it Right Now does what it says. To schedule, pick a date on the calendar, then click the clock to set a time, then confirm with Send Later. Check the time zone.

Scheduled emails freeze 24 hours ahead

While the status reads Planned, you can still make changes. At the 24-hour mark the status becomes Scheduled, and both the recipient list and the content lock. Anyone added to your case load after that point will not receive it.

To change a scheduled email, cancel it and reschedule rather than editing in place. Cancellations must happen at least 15 minutes before the send time; after that the email may go out as originally planned.

Send

The final step confirms your selections. Read the recipient count one more time. It should still look like the number you saw when you ran the query. Then send.

Afterward, the email's activity view shows opens, clicks, bounces, and unsubscribes. Unsubscribes are worth checking: someone leaving your list is a relationship signal, not just a statistic.

The short version

  • The list is the channel; the query is the audience. You need both, and the query does the real work.
  • Build the query first, always excluding the "No email" tag, and "No appeals" too if you are asking for anything.
  • Create your Email List 15 minutes ahead, name it for the donor's eyes, and reuse the same list for every send.
  • Send from your own name and address so replies reach you.
  • Publish after every edit. Virtuous sends the last published version, not what is on your screen.
  • Target by Contact Query, usually Primary and Secondary so both spouses hear from you.
  • Check the recipient count at the query and again at the final confirmation.
